# DESIGN.md — Oklahoma Christian University (OC)

## Brand
Oklahoma Christian University (OC) — Edmond, OK. NCAA DII "Eagles."
Tone: warm, professional, plain language. No hype, no exclamation points.

## Colors
- Maroon (primary): `#602633`
- Silver Gray (secondary/backgrounds): `#E9EAEA`
- Ink (body text): `#1F1B1C`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Use maroon for the nav bar, headings, and buttons. Keep backgrounds white or
silver gray so maroon stands out. Don't put maroon text on silver — use it
only on white or with white text on maroon.

## Typography
- Font: Inter (or system sans-serif fallback)
- Headings: bold/semibold
- Body: regular, 16px, sentence case (no all-caps paragraphs)

## Components
- Buttons: maroon background, white text, rounded corners (~6px)
- Cards: white background, thin light-gray border, rounded corners
- Nav bar: maroon background, white text and logo

## Rules of thumb
- Keep it simple — mostly white/gray space, maroon as the accent, not the fill
- One font family only
- Sentence case everywhere (headings, buttons, labels)
